Paste one player per line: the player's name, then the image link, and optionally a photo credit. Separate them with a tab or a comma. Copying the Player, Image link and Credit columns from the photo checklist and pasting here works directly.

Each photo is saved inside this browser where the image site allows it, so it also works offline. Photos the site won't let the file save are shown from the link instead, which needs an internet connection on the night.

Randomiser

Each round draws the same number of players at random from the Marquee, then from every other set in turn. Rounds repeat until every player in the pool has a slot, so stars and bargains are spread through the night.

Before we begin

Rules of the auction

  1. 1

    In this auction, there are 86 players up for bidding.

  2. 2

    Each team must assemble a squad of six players within a budget of ₹50 cr.

  3. 3

    There must be three batters, two bowlers and one wicketkeeper, consisting of three men and three women.

  4. 4

    Players are ranked 1–12 and will be presented for bidding in a random order. Their position will be stated alongside.

  5. 5

    To bid, raise your card and call your price. Any amount is allowed, as long as it beats the current bid.

  6. 6

    The highest bid wins the player. Once sold, a sale is final.

  7. 7

    You can’t bid more than the money you have left. However, you must also spend a minimum of ₹25 cr by the end of the auction.

  8. 8

    Each player scores Rank × price paid (in lakh). For example, a Rank 3 player bought for ₹2.5 cr scores 3 × 250 = 750.

  9. 9

    Your team’s score is the total for all 6 players.

  10. 10

    If you do not have a full quota of 6 players at the end of the game, you will be disqualified.

  11. 11

    The team with the lowest score at the end of the auction wins.
